NORTHGATE FINANCIAL SERVICES
HR HELPDESK: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Export generated from the HR knowledge base. Last updated: March 2025.

1. PAY AND PAYROLL

1.1 When am I paid?
Salary is paid monthly on the 27th. If the 27th falls on a weekend or a public holiday,
payment is made on the preceding working day. Payslips appear in the HR portal within 48
hours of the payment date.

1.2 My payslip looks wrong. What do I do?
Raise a payroll query in the HR portal within 10 working days of the payment date.
Corrections confirmed before the 15th of a month are paid in that month's run; anything
later is paid in the following run. Underpayments above EUR 500 can be paid off-cycle on
request.

1.3 How do I change my bank details?
Update them yourself in the HR portal. Changes made after the 15th of the month take
effect the following month, because payroll is locked from the 16th. You will receive a
confirmation email to your personal address as an anti-fraud check.

2. LEAVE AND ABSENCE

2.1 How much annual leave do I get?
25 days per year plus public holidays, accrued monthly. Part-time employees accrue pro
rata.

2.2 Can I carry leave into next year?
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over. Carried days expire on 31 March; there is no
payment in lieu for days that expire.

2.3 How much notice do I give for annual leave?
Two weeks for absences longer than three days; shorter absences need your manager's
agreement but no formal notice period. Requests during the year-end close period
(15 December to 5 January) need approval from your department head.

2.4 What do I do if I am sick?
Tell your manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence and log the absence in the HR
portal. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of absence.

2.5 What parental leave is available?
Maternity leave is five months at full pay. Paternity and second-parent leave is 20
working days at full pay, to be taken within six months of the birth or placement.
Shared parental arrangements are available; contact People Operations to model the
options before you decide.

3. WORKING ARRANGEMENTS

3.1 How many days do I work from the office?
Three days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday as team anchor days. Some roles are fully
remote by written agreement.

3.2 Can I work from another country temporarily?
Yes, for up to 20 working days per calendar year, in a country where the firm has a legal
entity or a recognised arrangement. It must be approved in advance by your manager and by
Tax, because working abroad can create a tax presence for the firm.

3.3 Can I change my working hours?
Flexible working requests are made in the HR portal. People Operations responds within 30
days. Changes to contracted hours are a contract variation and are confirmed in writing.

4. EXPENSES AND EQUIPMENT

4.1 What is the expense deadline?
Submit within 60 days of the spend. Itemised receipts are required for anything above
EUR 25. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll run.

4.2 What travel can I book?
Economy class for flights under six hours, premium economy above six hours. Rail is
preferred over air for journeys under four hours. All travel above EUR 1,500 needs
department head approval before booking.

4.3 My laptop is broken. Who do I contact?
The IT service desk at itsupport@northgate.example. Loan devices are available from
Facilities in the Milan office; remote employees are couriered a replacement within two
business days.

5. PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

5.1 How does the review cycle work?
There are two formal reviews per year: a mid-year check-in in June and a full review in
December. Objectives are set in January and may be revised at the mid-year point.

5.2 How long is probation?
Six months for permanent employees, with formal reviews at 30, 60 and 90 days. Notice
during probation is one month from either side.

5.3 What is the training budget?
EUR 1,500 per employee per year for external training, plus unlimited access to the
internal learning platform. Requests go to your manager, and anything leading to a
professional qualification also needs department head approval.

6. LEAVING THE FIRM

6.1 What notice do I have to give?
Three months for permanent employees after probation, one month during probation. Senior
roles may have a longer notice period stated in the contract.

6.2 What happens to my unused leave?
Accrued but untaken leave for the current year is paid in the final payroll run. Carried-
over days from the previous year are not paid.

6.3 When do I lose system access?
Access is revoked at 18:00 on your last working day. Export anything you personally need,
such as payslips and P60-equivalent documents, before then; People Operations can provide
copies afterwards on request.
