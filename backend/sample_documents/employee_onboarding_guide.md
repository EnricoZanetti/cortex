# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Northgate Financial Services: People Operations
Applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees joining after 1 January 2025.

## Before your first day

People Operations sends your contract and right-to-work request through the HR portal
once your offer is accepted. Return the signed contract and upload your identity
documents at least five working days before your start date so that IT can provision your
accounts in time.

Your manager will email you a first-week plan. If you have not received it 48 hours before
you start, contact peopleops@northgate.example.

## Day one

### Arrival and access

Arrive at the Milan office at 09:30 on your first day. Reception will issue a temporary
badge; your permanent access badge is produced during the afternoon and collected from
Facilities on the ground floor. Remote-first employees receive their laptop by courier
two working days before their start date and join the day-one session by video.

### Accounts and equipment

IT provisions your email, single sign-on and VPN before you arrive. On your first login
you must:

1. Set your single sign-on password (minimum 14 characters, not reused from any other
   service).
2. Enrol two multi-factor authentication methods; the authenticator app is mandatory,
   and a hardware key is required for anyone with production access.
3. Install the device management agent, which is what allows you to reach internal
   systems.

Hardware requests beyond the standard laptop, monitor and headset go through the IT
service desk and need manager approval.

## First week: mandatory training

Every new joiner must complete the following within ten working days. Access is assigned
automatically on your first day and completion is tracked by the Learning team.

- Information security and acceptable use
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) foundations
- Data protection and GDPR basics
- Code of conduct and speaking up
- Health and safety at work

Client-facing staff additionally complete the extended AML module and the conduct risk
module within thirty days. Your training dashboard shows what is outstanding.

## Your first 30, 60 and 90 days

Your manager sets objectives with you in week one and reviews them at 30, 60 and 90 days.
The 90-day review is the formal end of your probation period and its outcome is recorded
in the HR portal.

Probation is six months for permanent employees, and one month's notice applies during
this period from either side.

## Working arrangements

### Hybrid working

The standard arrangement is three days per week in the office, with Tuesday and Thursday
designated as team anchor days. Fully remote arrangements exist for specific roles and
must be agreed in writing with your manager and People Operations before your start date.

### Hours and flexibility

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours you may organise your day as
suits you and your team, provided your calendar reflects your availability.

## Pay, benefits and expenses

Salary is paid monthly on the 27th, or the preceding working day when the 27th falls on a
weekend or public holiday. Your first payslip appears in the HR portal within 48 hours of
the payment date.

Benefits enrolment opens on your start date and stays open for 30 days. If you miss the
window, the next opportunity is the annual enrolment period in November, unless you have a
qualifying life event.

Expenses are submitted in the finance portal within 60 days of the spend, with an itemised
receipt for anything above EUR 25. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll
run.

## Time off

The annual leave entitlement is 25 days plus public holidays, accrued monthly and
requested through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than
three days. Up to five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be used
by 31 March.

Sick leave is reported to your manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence. A medical
certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day.
