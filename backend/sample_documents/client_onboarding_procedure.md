# Client Onboarding Procedure

Northgate Financial Services — Procedure NGF-OPS-011
Owner: Client Operations · Applies to all new client relationships

## 1. Overview

This procedure describes the operational steps to take a prospective client from signed
mandate to a live, funded account on Northgate Vault. It sits underneath the AML Policy
(NGF-COMP-004): where the two documents differ on a due-diligence question, the AML Policy
prevails.

Target end-to-end time is ten business days for a standard-risk individual and twenty
business days for a corporate or high-risk relationship.

## 2. Stage 1 — Mandate and data collection

The relationship manager collects the signed mandate, the client's investment objectives
and the completed onboarding pack. The pack is incomplete without:

- Identity and address evidence for every account holder
- Corporate documents and the ownership structure, for entities
- Tax residency self-certification (CRS/FATCA) for every beneficial owner
- Source-of-wealth narrative where enhanced due diligence applies

Incomplete packs are returned within one business day. Do not open a case in the
onboarding queue until the pack is complete, because partial cases distort the service-
level reporting and lose their place in the queue when reopened.

## 3. Stage 2 — Screening and risk rating

Client Operations runs sanctions, PEP and adverse-media screening on every named party.
Any hit is escalated to Financial Crime Compliance the same day and the case is paused
until cleared.

The risk rating is produced by the onboarding scorecard, which considers jurisdiction,
product, delivery channel and expected volumes. Ratings are:

- **Low** — standard due diligence, review every 5 years
- **Medium** — standard due diligence, review every 2 years
- **High** — enhanced due diligence, senior management approval, review every 6 months

A rating may be overridden upward by Compliance at any time. It may only be overridden
downward by the MLRO, with a recorded rationale.

## 4. Stage 3 — Approval

Standard-risk cases are approved by the Client Operations team lead. High-risk cases and
all PEP relationships require approval from a member of senior management, evidenced in
the case record before the account is opened.

Approval is valid for 90 days. If the account is not opened within that window, screening
must be re-run.

## 5. Stage 4 — Account opening and provisioning

On approval, Client Operations opens the custody account, assigns the fee schedule from
the client agreement, and raises the Vault provisioning request. Provisioning takes one
business day and the client receives an activation email valid for 72 hours.

Entitlements are set at this point. Remember that dual authorisation is mandatory for cash
movements: the client must nominate at least one Instructor and one different Approver
before the account can transact.

## 6. Stage 5 — Funding

The account is funded by transfer from an account in the client's own name at a regulated
institution. Third-party funding is not accepted without prior Compliance approval and a
documented explanation of the relationship.

The first inbound payment is checked against the source-of-funds evidence gathered at
Stage 1. A material discrepancy — the amount, the originating institution or the
jurisdiction differing from what was declared — is escalated to the MLRO before the funds
are applied.

## 7. Stage 6 — Handover and first review

Once funded, the relationship manager runs a welcome session covering Vault navigation,
reporting options, instruction cut-off times and support channels.

The first relationship review takes place 90 days after funding, and confirms that actual
activity matches the expected activity declared at onboarding. Material divergence is a
trigger for a due-diligence refresh.

## 8. Service levels and escalation

| Step | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Pack completeness check | 1 business day |
| Screening | 2 business days |
| Compliance escalation clearance | 3 business days |
| Approval decision | 2 business days |
| Provisioning | 1 business day |

Cases breaching a target are flagged automatically in the daily operations report. Cases
open for more than 30 business days are escalated to the Head of Client Operations and
reported to the Operating Committee monthly.

## 9. Rejections

Where a relationship is declined, the relationship manager informs the client without
disclosing any financial-crime reasoning — see the tipping-off provisions of the AML
Policy. The reason is recorded in the case file for audit purposes and retained for five
years.
